      First Playdate? Here’s how it works
      1. Plug your UART adapter into your computer and attach it to your Paradise. Click Connect adapter and select its port.
      2. Choose a nickname. Join an open room, or create one and share its invite link. Rooms are private unless you choose to list them.
      3. Wait until both players are in the room with their adapters connected. Then, on both toys, open Menu → Connection → Playdate.
      4. Choose who presses B. Your choice updates both pages: one player presses once, the other waits. The screen can time out while waiting; reopen it on both toys if needed.
      5. Your ghosts and friendship appear in the scene as the toys exchange data.

      Adapter status confirms the browser connection. It can’t tell which menu is open on your toy.
